Cube Dance

A ring of cubes spins around a center cube that is waiting for a cue. Click an outer cube and the center cube picks up its rotation. Scale, speed, cube count, and colors are all live.

How it works

There is exactly one cube model: eight vertices arranged into 36 triangle vertices with a different color on each face. Every cube on screen is an instance of that model, drawn with its own transformation matrix.

  • Instancing. Each frame, JavaScript builds a model-view matrix per cube (translate, rotate about an arbitrary axis, scale) and uploads it as a uniform before drawing the shared mesh.
  • The ring. Outer cubes sit evenly on a circle of radius 0.75. Each one rotates about the line from the origin through its own center.
  • Picking by ray. A click is converted to normalized device coordinates, unprojected through the inverse view-projection matrix into a ray, and intersected with the plane the cubes live on. The nearest cube within range is selected.
  • Depth and perspective. A perspective camera looks at the scene with the depth buffer enabled, so faces of overlapping cubes are resolved correctly.
  • Extras. The outer ring can grow from 4 to 20 cubes, and the face colors can be re-rolled with random hues.

Background

Written for CSE 470, Introduction to Computer Graphics, at Arizona State University. Uses the course-supplied webgl-utils and MV.js helper libraries and unit-cube vertex data.
