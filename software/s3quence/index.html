S3QUENCE

S3QUENCE is currently under development

About

An original 2D arcade-combat game built around a chain of timed directional-input sequences, dungeon-room traversal, encounter-based progression, and run upgrades. The whole presentation is drawn through a custom CRT-style display layer.

Watch Prototype Source

How it works

S3QUENCE is a desktop Java game with no engine. A single GamePanel owns the loop, rendering, and state. Each frame it updates the current screen, polls keyboard and controller input, then draws the scene through a CRT compositor that can curve the image, overlay scanlines, and bleed color.

  • Screen States: Menu, settings, dungeon, shop, level-up, and combat encounters, are discrete states. The panel switches among them instead of stacking separate scenes.
  • Dungeon Rooms: You walk a generated room on a walkable path. Colliding with an enemy starts the combat sequence. Room transitions and intros are animated before the next space is live.
  • Sequences: An encounter builds a directional chain of varying length and style depending on the enemy. Each correct key adds damage scaled by how fast you entered it, restores a little time, and eases health drain. Be quick or suffer the consequences.
  • Enemy Rules: Enemy types keep gameplay varied and unexpected, forcing players to adapt their tactics and make split-second decisions that could mean life or death.
  • Progression: Player progression lives on an XP system where level-ups grant skill points; skill points are then used to purchase in-run items and upgrades to make yourself stronger than ever before and take on harder fights.
  • Audio & Haptics: Dynamic music, sound effects, and controller feedback reinforce every movement, attack, mistake, and victory, making each encounter feel responsive and impactful.

Simple Controls

  • Move. WASD or arrows in dungeon rooms. D-pad or left stick on a controller.
  • Sequence. Arrow keys enter the encounter chain. The same directions come from the stick or D-pad.
  • Confirm. Enter, or A / X / Start.
  • Back. Escape, or B / Y / Back.
