About

Drawn first.
Engineered after.

I am Randy Mattoka, a digital illustrator and Computer Science student at ASU.

The pictures come from comics: hard shapes, lighting, and atmosphere. The software side is the same instinct aimed at systems — how a world holds together, how a player moves through it, how an image becomes an experience. These are not separate careers parked on one domain. They are the same studio.
