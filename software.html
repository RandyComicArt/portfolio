I am a Computer Science student at Arizona State University. I build software, interactive experiences, tools, and games — often in conversation with the same worlds I draw. The graphics work below runs live in your browser, and larger projects will be added alongside it.

Interactive Graphics

Graphics Experiments

Four WebGL programs written in plain JavaScript and GLSL, with no engine or framework. They began as coursework for CSE 470, Computer Graphics at Arizona State University. Pick one and launch it: each runs live in the page.

4 experiments · WebGL · Academic projects

Launch demo

01

Petal Burst

A flower made of 32 solid-colored triangles that bursts apart and fades to white while its core holds still. The motion and the color change are both computed in the vertex shader.

Interaction
Plays automatically and loops
Concepts
Vertex shader animation, uniforms, color interpolation
  • WebGL
  • GLSL
  • JavaScript

02

Cube Dance

One cube model drawn as a ring of spinning instances around a center cube. Click an outer cube and the center cube picks up its rotation axis, then tune scale, speed, cube count, and color.

Interaction
Click cubes, sliders, and buttons
Concepts
Instancing, model-view matrices, ray picking
  • WebGL
  • GLSL
  • JavaScript
Launch demo Source
Launch demo

03

Surfaces of Revolution

A cylinder and a wavy vase generated from curves, with exact normals and per-pixel Phong lighting. Orbit them, swap materials, move the light, and push the shininess from matte to mirror.

Interaction
Drag to orbit, plus material, light, shininess, and field-of-view controls
Concepts
Parametric surfaces, indexed meshes, Phong shading
  • WebGL
  • GLSL
  • JavaScript
Launch demo Source
Launch demo

04

Dino Walk

A Yoshi-inspired dinosaur built from copies of a single cube and animated through a hierarchical model, walking a looping path across a textured ground plane.

Interaction
Walks automatically; pause and reset buttons
Concepts
Hierarchical modeling, texture mapping, Blinn-Phong lighting
  • WebGL
  • GLSL
  • JavaScript
